# 🎯 PropCheck - Trading Simulator

An interactive trading simulator designed for prop firm challenges that helps traders practice risk management, develop discipline, and avoid common psychological pitfalls like revenge trading.

## 🚀 Live Demo

**[Try PropCheck Now](https://vaggelisgian.github.io/PropCheck/)**

## ✨ Features

### 📊 Real-Time Trading Simulation
- Start with customizable account balance ($10,000 - $200,000)
- Set your daily loss limit (5% - 20%)
- Real-time P&L tracking
- Win rate and profit factor statistics

### 🎯 Golden Zone Analysis
- Intelligent risk assessment for each trade
- Visual feedback with color-coded zones:
  - 🟢 **Green Zone**: Safe trading territory
  - 🟡 **Yellow Zone**: Caution - approaching limits
  - 🔴 **Red Zone**: High risk - consider stopping
- Dynamic recommendations based on your current trading state

### 🛡️ Tilt Prevention System
- Real-time psychological state monitoring
- Early warning system for revenge trading tendencies
- Actionable advice to maintain discipline
- Trade streak tracking to identify emotional patterns

### 📈 Performance Metrics
- Total P&L tracking
- Win rate percentage
- Profit factor calculation
- Daily drawdown monitoring
- Trade history log

## 🎮 How to Use

1. **Configure Your Account**
   - Set your starting balance
   - Define your daily loss limit
   - Click "Start Trading Day"

2. **Execute Trades**
   - Enter your trade P&L (positive for wins, negative for losses)
   - Click "Log Trade" to record each transaction
   - Monitor your Golden Zone status in real-time

3. **Stay Disciplined**
   - Watch the tilt indicator for psychological warnings
   - Follow the recommendations provided
   - Reset daily to practice consistency

4. **Review Performance**
   - Check your win rate and profit factor
   - Analyze your trade history
   - Identify patterns in your trading behavior

## 📖 Trading Psychology Features

### Golden Zone System
The Golden Zone helps you understand where you stand in your trading day:
- Tracks cumulative P&L against your daily limit
- Provides visual feedback on risk exposure
- Suggests actions based on current zone status

### Tilt Detection
The tilt indicator monitors:
- Consecutive losses
- Large loss amounts relative to balance
- Rapid-fire trading after losses
- Deviation from normal trading patterns
